# Device acceptance checks

Firmware 1.0.9 was USB-flashed with esptool hash verification. Hardware checks observe connected Wi-Fi/SNTP, advancing live quotes, 500 ms Nixie frame timing, history fetch/save and a successful OTA manifest check without watchdog resets; the final measurement is in [the device report](../test/device_refresh_report.json). All five frequency settings and all 21 chart buckets at each range pass sanitized host tests. Firmware 1.0.7 was withdrawn after physical TLS watchdog failures. Five-detent calibration, visible output, full-size 24-hour concurrent TLS memory and actual power-loss/bootloader rollback remain unverified. See [refresh behavior and reproduction](NIXIE_REFRESH.md). These remaining checks require the assembled clock; modeled hardware cannot certify analog resistor tolerances or visible tube output.

1. Flash the complete initial PlatformIO build to the ESP32's explicit serial port. Confirm `status` shows firmware, heap and Wi-Fi state. Run `selftest`; confirm all six grids and orientation match the demo.
2. Move each five-position selector through every position. Run `dials` at each detent. Update the two independent mV calibration arrays if needed; verify each position's logged interval/window and verify contact transitions do not select another position.
3. With blank credentials, verify the matrix shows `CONNECT` over `WIFI`, with long text scrolling, and a single zero travels across the six Nixies at 100 ms per tube. Join the passwordless `BitcoinClock-xxxxxx` setup AP and open 192.168.4.1. Verify the network picker lists nearby networks by signal, collapses duplicate SSIDs, and refreshes with **Scan again**. Selecting an open network hides the password field; secured networks ask for a password; **Other network…** accepts hidden SSIDs. Try a wrong home Wi-Fi password; verify the matrix changes to `CONNECTING`, then `WIFI / FAILED`, setup stays open and credentials are not saved. Connect successfully; verify the matrix and page report success, the zero chase stops, and live prices begin. AP, DNS and port 80 disappear after eight seconds and the chart returns. Power-cycle and verify automatic reconnection. Check BOOT-hold recovery and `wifi-reset`; reopened setup must also be passwordless and show the setup text/animation. When upgrading, verify old setup passwords are removed and saved home Wi-Fi still reconnects.
4. Compare a logged price to its logged exchange, allowing differences between sources. Observe all eleven providers over several rotations. Confirm USDT prices use the logged conversion; disable/block Kraken and wait two minutes to confirm USD-only fallback. Check Nixie whole-dollar rounding and blanking on a long outage.
5. Check JSEL2/GPIO35 frequency detents (0.5 s, 2 s, 30 s, 5 min, 1 h) and JSEL1/GPIO34 range detents (5 min, 30 min, 1 h, 24 h, 7 d). Change every chart scale. Confirm genuine candles arrive, rightmost live-price motion depends on the selected vertical scale, and large weekly moves change the graph. Turn off Internet access and restore it; dated history may remain, Nixies blank when stale, and normal operation resumes. After each range first fetches, confirm `status` reports a saved epoch. Return to it immediately: the cached chart should render without another history download. Restart and confirm `History restored` precedes market requests. In the weekly range, power off for two days: the five-day overlap must appear at its original timestamps while only the missing two days are fetched. On a development unit, interrupt a cache write and verify the previous complete snapshot remains usable.
6. Use `ticks` to log every tube frame. At each frequency, confirm logged intervals and that `status` reports a stable frequency selector at the intended position. The 0.5-second setting should send frames roughly every 500 ms with normal loop jitter, during history requests. At 0.5 s, real differing quotes are preferred; cosmetic rendering can move at most $1 from the selected real quote. Check raw quote, actual `digits` and `cosmetic` flag. Slower positions use ordinary rounding; chart/history must not store cosmetic values. Slow settings must hold the previous sampled value between deadlines. Change the frequency during an active request and confirm the next frame adopts it immediately. Confirm `stream=connected`, quote counters advance, and REST resumes on stream loss. Repeat with a full 24-hour history download to measure concurrent TLS/JSON memory. An unconfirmed selector defaults to 0.5 seconds; measure/calibrate its five voltages rather than assuming that fallback confirms the wiring.
   At the 0.5-second setting, inspect heap/minimum heap and long-running behavior during a 24-hour history fetch, Wi-Fi loss/recovery and AP reopening. `status` exposes heap and largest allocation; real TLS memory must be measured on-device.
   At startup with a saved chart and with an initially empty cache, verify the matrix fills every LED left to right and top to bottom, shows a fully lit frame, then clears in the same order while keeping the selected chart pixels lit. Each pass should take about 0.82 seconds. Check live prices and both selectors remain responsive during the reveal. Later range changes and reopening Wi-Fi setup must not restart the reveal.
7. Publish a newer signed test firmware from clean `main`. Confirm the device installs only after `ota/manifest.json` changes on `main`, not merely after a source commit or another branch commit. Verify successful boot health confirmation and persisted Wi-Fi.
8. On a development device with USB recovery available, interrupt an OTA download/network connection. Confirm the active firmware remains usable. Repeat with controlled power interruption and a deliberately crashing/unhealthy signed test image to verify the actual bootloader rolls back. Check wrong-board manifests, signatures, corrupted binaries and old versions are rejected.

The Nixie hardware's high-voltage power is not controlled by this firmware; blank tubes are a display state. The driver uses the working demo's normal-current `0xAA` packet header and brightness 64.
