# Device acceptance checks

The host suites and ESP32 compilation are complete, and USB uploads have passed esptool flash-hash verification on the attached clock. These remaining checks need the assembled ESP32 clock. A modeled network/flash cannot certify physical Wi-Fi reception, flash power-loss behavior, analog resistor tolerances or the visible display.

1. Flash the complete initial PlatformIO build to the ESP32's explicit serial port. Confirm `status` shows firmware, heap and Wi-Fi state. Run `selftest`; confirm all six grids and orientation match the demo.
2. Move each five-position selector through every position. Run `dials` at each detent. Update the two independent mV calibration arrays if needed; verify each position's logged interval/window and verify contact transitions do not select another position.
3. With blank credentials, verify the matrix shows `CONNECT` over `WIFI`, with long text scrolling, and a single zero travels across the six Nixies at 100 ms per tube. Join the passwordless `BitcoinClock-xxxxxx` setup AP and open 192.168.4.1. Try a wrong home Wi-Fi password; verify the matrix changes to `CONNECTING`, then `WIFI / FAILED`, setup stays open and credentials are not saved. Connect successfully; verify the matrix and page report success, the zero chase stops, and live prices begin. AP, DNS and port 80 disappear after eight seconds and the chart returns. Power-cycle and verify automatic reconnection. Check BOOT-hold recovery and `wifi-reset`; reopened setup must also be passwordless and show the setup text/animation. When upgrading, verify old setup passwords are removed and saved home Wi-Fi still reconnects.
4. Compare a logged price to its logged exchange, allowing differences between sources. Observe all eleven providers over several rotations. Confirm USDT prices use the logged conversion; disable/block Kraken and wait two minutes to confirm USD-only fallback. Check Nixie whole-dollar rounding and blanking on a long outage.
5. Change every chart scale. Confirm genuine candles arrive, rightmost live-price motion depends on the selected vertical scale, and large weekly/monthly moves change the graph. Turn off Internet access and restore it; dated history may remain, Nixies blank when stale, and normal operation resumes.
6. At the two-second setting, inspect heap/minimum heap and long-running behavior during a yearly history fetch, Wi-Fi loss/recovery and AP reopening. `status` exposes heap and largest allocation; real TLS memory must be measured on-device.
7. Publish a newer signed test firmware from clean `main`. Confirm the device installs only after `ota/manifest.json` changes on `main`, not merely after a source commit or another branch commit. Verify successful boot health confirmation and persisted Wi-Fi.
8. On a development device with USB recovery available, interrupt an OTA download/network connection. Confirm the active firmware remains usable. Repeat with controlled power interruption and a deliberately crashing/unhealthy signed test image to verify the actual bootloader rolls back. Check wrong-board manifests, signatures, corrupted binaries and old versions are rejected.

The Nixie hardware's high-voltage power is not controlled by this firmware; blank tubes are a display state. The driver uses the working demo's normal-current `0xAA` packet header and brightness 64.
